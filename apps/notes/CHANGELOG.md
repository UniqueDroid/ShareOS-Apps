## 1.1.0

- First version in the ShareOS app store.
- ShareOS look in light and dark (colours of the theme, accent colour); the note colours have a light and a dark set.
- Notes of version 1.0 keep their colour (the stored dark colour becomes its name).
- Saves only what changed; a note emptied in the dialog is deleted; deleting asks first.
- A click outside the new note, Esc or Ctrl+Enter closes it and keeps it.
- Edited notes move to the top.
- Clear notices when the account has no file access or no writable folder; the notes then stay in the browser.
- Removed: "opens" for `.json`, `.txt`, `.md` from the file browser (the app did not handle an opened file yet).

## 1.0.3

- Notes open with a click on the card in an edit dialog: title, text and colour, saved on the box.
- Close with a click outside, Esc or "Schließen".
